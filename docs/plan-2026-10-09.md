## The vision, challenged

| Your point | Agree? | The catch |
|---|---|---|
| Projections for every player | Yes, we have them | A point estimate isn't enough. Ours overstate gaps (margins realize at 0.84), and without an error band the ranking flips on noise. Every number needs its band, and a gap inside the band is a tie |
| Exact schedule, optimal lineups each night | Yes, done (daily exact solver) | Goalie starts are probabilities; opponents don't always set their best lineup, and they stream. Both are logged, not yet modeled |
| Predict this week's and the next matchups | Half done | Only this week is played against the real opponent. Later weeks are a generic "typical week" times the season. The next 2 opponents and schedules are known, so play those weeks explicitly too |
| Adds have a cost | Done: the add price, in win probability | Set by simulation on 2 weeks of candidates, so provisional until mid-November |
| Maximize the season: title, playoffs, seed | Yes, as the goal | Not as the per-move score: title odds per move are pure noise, and the sim (2026-10-03) found weighting adds by standings leverage *lowered* title odds. The currency should be **expected wins** (sum of P(win) over the weeks a move touches), with 6 adds kept for the playoffs. Playoff and title odds are shown as context. Seed matters only in weeks 20-23 |
| One source of truth, a clear log | Yes | Today inputs, derived numbers and message bookkeeping share one file |
| A continuously ranked add/drop table | **Yes, this is the product** | A table of single moves isn't a plan: the best move may be best made Monday, and two moves interact. So the table has a "when" column, and the recommendation is a rule applied to the table, never a separate calculation |
| Crisp messages, nicer visuals | Yes | Telegram is for the action, the dashboard for evaluating. At most one image a message, made from the same design as the dashboard |

## The design: data -> Board -> rule -> views

```
inputs (ledger)  ->  Board (one per run, archived)  ->  rule (recommend / keep / change)  ->  Telegram, dashboard
```

**1. Inputs, one ledger.** What happened, append-only: my moves and the league's
(Transactions, Done taps), screenshot reads, Yahoo scores. Rosters by day
are derived from it, never edited by hand. Message bookkeeping (pending cards,
Telegram offset) lives apart from facts.

**2. The Board** (`state/board.json`, plus each run's copy in the private archive),
the only thing messages and the dashboard read:
- *Header:* week, opponent, score so far, expected (with band), P(win), adds left
  (week/season), playoff and title odds.
- *Matchups* for this week and the next two: opponent, both teams' expected
  points and P(win), each night's lineup games for both teams (open slots by
  position), the goalie minimum.
- *The move table*, every add/drop weighed:

| add | drop | when | wk 2 | wk 3 | wk 4 | later | Δ wins | band | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tolvanen | Samuelsson | today | +5.2 pts, 16->21% | ... | ... | +9 pts | +0.13 | ±? | clears the price |

  (Today's numbers; the band comes from step 2.)

  Δ wins = the change in P(win) in each of the 3 explicit weeks + the later
  points (shrunk by how well they predict) at a typical week's worth of a point.
  The add price is in the same unit (an add's expected wins elsewhere).
- *The plan:* the committed moves, each dated, with why, and what it replaced.

**3. The rule.**
- Make a move when its Δ wins clears the add price (as now), on the day it
  starts paying ("when"). A move that pays nothing until Monday waits for Monday.
- Two moves within each other's band are a tie. Ties go to this week's gain,
  then next week's.
- The committed plan stays unless it can't be done (player taken, injured, Nico
  did something else) or a new one beats it by the margin (**D2**). A change
  is said once, with its reason ("Celebrini to IR: Kelly -> Tolvanen, +0.04 wins").

**4. Views.**
- *Telegram plan*, 4-6 lines:
  ```
  Wk 2 vs Retrot: 137-163, expect 223-250. Win 16%.
  Do today: Tolvanen for Samuelsson (16% -> 21%, keeps value).
  Then: nothing until Mon. Wk 3 vs <opp>: 48%, a 2-add week.
  Adds: 1 this week, 33 season. Playoffs 21%.
  [image: next 7 nights, both lineups] [dashboard link]
  ```
- *A card* per move on the day it's due, sent once. A replaced card is edited to
  "Replaced: ..." and its buttons go.
- *A screenshot* updates the score in the plan message; new cards only if the plan changed.
- *The dashboard* is the place to evaluate: the 3 matchups night by night,
  the full move table (sortable, with bands), the plan and its history, the
  season path (adds used vs pace, standings odds).
